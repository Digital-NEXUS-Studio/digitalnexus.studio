1. What KerfCraft does

KerfCraft calculates cutting layouts for sheet goods and linear stock from the sizes, quantities, saw kerf and settings you enter. It shows cut diagrams, offcut estimates and costs, and exports cut sheets as PDF, parts lists as CSV and backups as JSON. It works offline and needs no account.

2. Check before you cut

  • Layouts, yields and costs are computed estimates. Their accuracy depends on the measurements and settings you enter, and real material can differ (warped or damaged sheets, grain direction, actual blade width).
  • Check dimensions and the plan yourself before cutting. Digital NEXUS is not responsible for wasted material caused by incorrect input or by relying on a plan without checking it.
  • KerfCraft gives no safety guidance. Always follow your tool manufacturer's instructions and safe workshop practice.
