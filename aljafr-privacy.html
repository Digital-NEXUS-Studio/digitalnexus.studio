1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the use of كتاب الجفر - Al Jafr, developed and distributed by Digital NEXUS. We are committed to transparency and to protecting your personal data.

2. Cultural & Educational Scope (Historical Literature Notice)

Al Jafr is strictly an educational reader and historical literary reference tool covering classical Islamic manuscripts and traditional Arabic abjad letter-value calculation. The application does NOT provide divination, fortune-telling, occult forecasting, or supernatural claims. It is provided solely for academic, historical, and cultural study.

3. Data Collection, Transmission & Usage

Al-Jafr requires no account or registration. Any names, dates, places, or questions you type for abjad calculation are processed locally on your device during your session and are never sent to Digital NEXUS servers (one tap clears them from the app).

The free Android version uses Google AdMob for ads and Google Play Billing for the optional one-time Al-Jafr Pro (aljafr_pro) upgrade, which removes all ads and unlocks unlimited daily readings and name comparisons.

4. Permissions & Device Access

Al-Jafr does not access contacts, location, camera, or microphone. Internet access, network state, and the Android Advertising ID permission are used by Google AdMob to load consent forms and ads in the free version, and by Google Play Billing to verify and restore Al-Jafr Pro.

5. Third-Party Services & Libraries

  • Google AdMob (Advertising & UMP Consent): The free version shows interstitial ads between reading flows (never in the first 60 seconds and at most once every 3 minutes) and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for an extra daily reading. To show, cap, and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google: your device's advertising ID (Device or other IDs), IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app diagnostics/information, and how you interact with ads. Google acts as an independent controller under the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses partner-site data. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form asks for your consent first; you can update your choice anytime via Ad privacy choices inside the app or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings. Purchasing Al-Jafr Pro disables all ads and consent requests.
  • Google Play Billing: If you purchase Al-Jafr Pro, payment is handled by Google Play. We never receive your card details. Google's billing library sends technical diagnostics to Google, confirms entitlement to the app, and provides Digital NEXUS a Play order record (order number, date, price, country) used only for accounting, refunds, and support.

6. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

We rely on HTTPS/TLS encryption for network requests made by Google Play and Google AdMob. Local app settings and cached session state stay in sandbox app storage on your device and can be cleared anytime in the app or Android Settings. You can also contact us below regarding any support inquiry or Play order record.

7. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for adult and professional users (age 18 and older) and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
