1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the use of فراسة - Firasah, developed and distributed by Digital NEXUS. We are committed to transparency and to protecting your personal data.

2. Cultural & Educational Scope (Non-Divination Notice)

Firasah is strictly an educational and cultural reference application exploring historical Arabic traditions of non-verbal expression and classical physiognomic literature. It does NOT claim or provide fortune-telling, divination, predictive astrology, or medical/psychiatric diagnosis. All insights are presented strictly for cultural, historical, and self-reflection purposes.

3. Data Collection, Transmission & Usage

Camera capture and facial landmark measurement run 100% on your device. No biometric data, facial scans, camera streams, or personal photos are ever uploaded, transmitted, or stored on Digital NEXUS servers.

You do not need an account. The free Android version displays ads via Google AdMob and uses Google Play Billing for the optional one-time Firasah Pro (firasah_pro) upgrade, which removes all ads and unlocks full readings.

4. Permissions & Device Access

Camera access is used solely for on-device facial proportion analysis (no frames are uploaded). Internet access, network state, and the Android Advertising ID permission are used by Google AdMob to load consent forms and ads in the free version, and by Google Play Billing to process and restore Firasah Pro.

5. Third-Party Services & Libraries

  • Google AdMob (Advertising & UMP Consent): The free version shows interstitial ads between sessions/results (never in the first 60 seconds and at most once every 3 minutes, never during camera capture) and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch to unlock an extra daily reading. To deliver, cap, and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google: your device's advertising ID (Device or other IDs), IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app diagnostics/information, and how you interact with ads. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses partner-site data. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form asks for your consent first; you can update your choice anytime using Ad privacy choices inside the app or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings. Purchasing Firasah Pro disables all ads and consent requests.
  • Google Play Billing: If you buy Firasah Pro, payment is processed directly by Google Play. We never see your card details. Google's billing library sends technical diagnostic information to Google, tells the app your purchase status, and gives Digital NEXUS a Play order record (order number, date, price, country) used only for accounting, refunds, and support.

6. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

We rely on HTTPS/TLS encryption for network requests made by Google Play and Google AdMob. Your saved readings and settings are stored only on your phone and can be deleted inside the app (or by clearing app data / uninstalling). Contact us below for any billing or support inquiry.

7. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for adult and professional users (age 18 and older) and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
