KerfCraft: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026
Developer: Digital NEXUS (digitalnexus.studio)
Package: com.kerfcraft.app

1. Summary

KerfCraft is a cut-list optimizer for sheet goods and linear stock. Your projects stay on your phone. Digital NEXUS does not collect, receive, sell or share your data. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics. Purchases of KerfCraft Pro and the optional rating prompt are handled by Google Play; Google's billing component sends technical diagnostic data to Google, as described in section 4.

2. What the app stores

The app saves in its private storage on your device:

  • Your projects: names, materials, stock sizes and prices, part lists and cut settings.
  • Your Scrap Vault (saved offcuts) and preferences such as units and saw kerf.
  • Whether KerfCraft Pro is unlocked, with the Google Play purchase token used to confirm it.
  • Simple counters that decide when Google Play's rating prompt may appear (number of successful exports and the date of the last prompt).

The app never sends this information to us or to anyone else.

3. What we collect

Nothing. KerfCraft has no user accounts and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools of our own. We do not receive your projects, files, location, contacts or any other personal information.

4. Google Play services

KerfCraft uses components provided by Google. Data they handle is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by us.

  • Google Play Billing (buying and restoring KerfCraft Pro). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. To operate and improve the billing service, Google's Play Billing Library also sends technical diagnostic information to Google, such as the results and timing of billing requests, the app and library versions, and device information.
  • Google Play In-App Review. After you have successfully used the app, Google Play may occasionally show its standard rating window. Any rating or review you submit goes to Google Play.
  • No location access. Google's billing library includes a Google Play services location component, but KerfCraft does not request the location permission, so neither the app nor that component can access your location.

5. Permissions

The app uses the Internet and network-state permissions only so Google Play Billing can check and complete purchases, plus Google Play's billing permission. It asks for no other permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.

6. Files you export or import

When you export a cut-sheet PDF, a parts CSV or a JSON backup, the file is created on your device and shared only to the destination you choose in Android's share menu (for example email, a cloud drive or a printer). When you import a CSV or backup, the app reads only the file you pick. The app or service you share to is covered by its own privacy policy.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete projects, parts and scraps in the app. Clearing the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of its data from your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may keep a copy of the app's data in your own Google account backup, which you manage in your Google account. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; you can still write to us with any question.

8. Children

KerfCraft is a workshop tool for general audiences and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
