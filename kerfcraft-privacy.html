KerfCraft: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Developer: Digital NEXUS (digitalnexus.studio)
Package: com.kerfcraft.app

1. Summary

KerfCraft is a cut-list optimizer for sheet goods and linear stock. Your projects stay on your phone. Digital NEXUS does not collect, receive, sell or share your projects. The free version shows Google AdMob ads (interstitials after solving/exporting, plus optional rewarded video ads for a clean watermark-free PDF export). Purchases of the optional one-time KerfCraft Pro upgrade remove all ads and unlock all features through Google Play Billing; Google services handle ad delivery diagnostics, consent, and billing diagnostics as described in section 4.

2. What the app stores

The app saves in its private storage on your device:

  • Your projects: names, materials, stock sizes and prices, part lists and cut settings.
  • Your Scrap Vault (saved offcuts) and preferences such as units and saw kerf.
  • Whether KerfCraft Pro is unlocked, with the Google Play purchase token used to confirm it.
  • Simple counters that decide when Google Play's rating prompt may appear (number of successful exports and the date of the last prompt).

The app never sends this information to us or to anyone else.

3. What we collect

Digital NEXUS has no user accounts and does not collect your projects, files, location or contacts on our own servers. When you use the free version, Google AdMob and Google Play services handle advertising ID, IP address, diagnostics, and purchase data as described below.

4. Google services

KerfCraft uses components provided by Google. Data they handle is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by us.

  • Google AdMob (Advertising & UMP Consent). The free version shows paced interstitial ads after optimisation or export (never in the first 60 seconds and at most once every 3 minutes) and optional rewarded video ads on tap to unlock a watermark-free PDF export. To serve, cap, and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google: your advertising ID (Device or other IDs), IP address (approximate location), app/device diagnostics, and ad interactions. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form runs first, and you can update your choice anytime via Ad privacy choices in Settings (or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings). Buying KerfCraft Pro disables all ads and consent requests.
  • Google Play Billing (buying and restoring KerfCraft Pro). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. To operate and improve the billing service, Google's Play Billing Library sends technical diagnostic information to Google (such as billing request results, timing, app/library versions, and device information) and gives Digital NEXUS a Play order record (purchase history: order number, date, price, country) used only for accounting, refunds, and support.
  • Google Play In-App Review. After you have successfully used the app, Google Play may occasionally show its standard rating window. Any rating or review you submit goes to Google Play.
  • No location access. Google's billing library includes a Google Play services location component, but KerfCraft does not request the location permission, so neither the app nor that component can access your location.

5. Permissions

The app uses the Internet, network-state, Advertising ID, and Google Play billing permissions so Google Play Billing can check/complete purchases, Google Play Review can run, and Google AdMob/UMP can serve and manage consent for free-tier ads. It asks for no other permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.

6. Files you export or import

When you export a cut-sheet PDF, a parts CSV or a JSON backup, the file is created on your device and shared only to the destination you choose in Android's share menu (for example email, a cloud drive or a printer). When you import a CSV or backup, the app reads only the file you pick. The app or service you share to is covered by its own privacy policy.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete projects, parts and scraps in the app. Clearing the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of its data from your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may keep a copy of the app's data in your own Google account backup, which you manage in your Google account. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; you can still write to us with any question.

8. Children

KerfCraft is a workshop tool for general audiences and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
