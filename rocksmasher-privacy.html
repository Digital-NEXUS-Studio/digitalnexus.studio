Official Game Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy — Rock Smasher 3D

Effective Date: November 25, 2026
Developer / Publisher: Digital NEXUS
Package Identifier: com.rocksmasher.nexus

1. Overview

Rock Smasher 3D is a 3D shape-matching arcade game developed and published by Digital NEXUS. You do not need an account. Digital NEXUS does not collect or receive any personal information from the game.

2. Game data on your device

Scores, progress and sound settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between levels (at most one every few minutes, never during play), and rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for a shield to retry a failed level.

To show, limit and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects and shares with Google: your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google acts as an independent controller of this data. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. In-app purchase (Rock Smasher Pro)

The optional one-time Rock Smasher Pro (rocksmasher_pro) purchase removes automatic ads between levels (optional rewarded ads for a shield remain on tap only). Payment is handled through Google Play; Digital NEXUS never sees your card details. Google Play Billing sends technical diagnostic information to Google and provides Digital NEXUS a Play order record (order number, date, price, country) used only for accounting, refunds and support.

5. Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy settings on the game's main menu. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Privacy → Ads), which also limits personalised ads.

6. Children

Rock Smasher 3D is not directed at children under 13, and Digital NEXUS does not knowingly collect information from children.
